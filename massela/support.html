Massela is a charades-style party game: hold the phone to your forehead, your friends act out the word, and you guess it. Words come in English and Arabic. Need a hand? Start here. If your answer is not on this page, email us.

How to play

  1. On the home screen, tap the packs you want to play. Tap a pack again to deselect it, or use Select All and Deselect All at the top.
  2. Tap Play. The phone switches to landscape.
  3. Hold the phone against your forehead with the screen facing your friends. The round starts when the phone is upright, or tap anywhere on the screen to start.
  4. Your friends act out the word shown. Tilt the phone up when you guess it, or down to pass.
  5. When the timer runs out you see your score and every word. Tap Play Again for another round or Menu to change packs.

The tutorial is always one tap away: tap the ? icon on the home screen.

Tilting does not work

  • Hold it upright. Keep the phone vertical in landscape, against your forehead. Lying flat on a table will not start a round. You can always tap the screen to start instead.
  • Tilt clearly, then come back. Tilt up for correct or down to pass, then bring the phone back to upright before the next tilt.
  • Wait for the flash. After each answer the screen flashes green or red for a moment, and tilts during the flash are ignored.

Pausing and leaving a round

  • Tap the pause button in the bottom corner of the screen. On Android, the back button pauses too.
  • The round pauses by itself if you get a phone call or switch to another app.
  • From the pause screen you can resume, change sound and music, or exit to the menu.

No sound or music

Open Settings (the gear icon on the home screen) and make sure Sound Effects and Music are on, then check your phone's volume. You can also switch them from the pause screen during a round.

Round length

In Settings, drag Game Duration to choose a round from 30 to 120 seconds, in 15 second steps.

Your own packs

  • Under My Packs, tap Create Pack. Give it a title, pick a cover photo or a color, and add your words in English, Arabic or both. Then tap Save Pack.
  • To edit a pack, tap the pencil on it or press and hold the pack. Inside the editor you can remove words, change the cover, or delete the whole pack with the trash button.
  • Cover photos are chosen through your phone's standard photo picker. Massela only sees the photo you pick.

Your data

Massela has no accounts and collects no personal data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Your settings and custom packs live only on your phone and are removed when you delete the app. Read the full Privacy Policy.
