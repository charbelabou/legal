Kezbe Bayda — Support

Kezbe Bayda is a Lebanese Arabizi bluffing trivia party game for 3 to 8 phones on the same Wi-Fi. Need a hand? Start here. If your answer is not on this page, email us.

How to play

  1. One phone taps HOST A GAME and shows a room code.
  2. Everyone else taps JOIN A GAME and types the code.
  3. Each round shows a trivia question with a blank. Write a fake answer that sounds real.
  4. All the answers, the lies and the truth, are shuffled together. Vote for the one you think is true.
  5. Find the truth: +1000 points. Fool a friend with your lie: +500 for each one. Most points at the end wins.

You need at least 3 players and at most 8. Playing alone? Tap Practice with bots on the home screen to play a full show against four computer players.

Phones cannot find each other or join the room

  • Same network. Every phone must be on the same Wi-Fi network as the host, or on the host's phone hotspot. Mobile data on its own will not work.
  • Local Network permission (iPhone and iPad). The first time you host or join, iOS asks to find devices on your local network. Tap Allow. If you tapped Don't Allow, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and turn on Kezbe Bayda.
  • Guest, office and school networks. Some networks stop devices from talking to each other, which blocks the game. Use your home Wi-Fi, or turn on a hotspot on one phone and connect the others to it.
  • Check the code. The room code is 4 letters on most home networks and 6 letters on larger networks. Type exactly what the host's screen shows.
  • Still stuck? Close the room, force-quit the app on every phone, turn Wi-Fi off and on, and host again.

Game language

Questions and answers are in Lebanese Arabizi by default. The host can switch the whole room to English in the room settings before the show starts.

Reporting a problem or a player

  • In the app, open Settings → Report a problem to send us an email.
  • Slurs and profanity are blocked automatically. The host can also remove a player from the lobby with the × next to their name.
  • Found a wrong answer or a mistake in a question? Email us the question and we will fix it.

Your data

Kezbe Bayda has no accounts and collects no personal data, so there is nothing to delete on our side. Your player name and settings live only on your phone and are removed when you delete the app. Read the full Privacy Policy.
