Kezbe Bayda — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Kezbe Bayda is a party trivia game played on phones over the same Wi-Fi network. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we do not collect your personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Kezbe Bayda has no accounts, no backend servers, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We never receive your name, answers, or any other information from the game.

What stays on your device

The app saves your player name and your settings (such as sound, haptics and motion preferences) on your own device. This data is never sent to us. Deleting the app removes it.

What is shared with other players

When you host or join a game room, your player name, your answers and your votes are sent directly between the phones in the room over your local network. This traffic does not go through any server of ours, and the room's content disappears when the game ends. Players in the same room can see the name you chose, so avoid using your real name if you prefer not to share it.

Permissions

  • Local network access (iOS) / network and Wi-Fi state (Android): used only to find and connect to other phones in your game room. No data leaves your network for this purpose.
  • Audio: the app plays music and sound effects. It does not record audio, and it does not use your camera or location.

App updates

The app can download updates to its game content and code from Expo's update service (expo.dev). Like any internet request, this may expose your IP address and basic device and app version information to that service. No personal data from the game is included. See Expo's privacy policy.

Children

Kezbe Bayda is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
